### Build notes

to avoid desoldering tasks..

the log leg is positive - which means the pin is on top.

ignore the red color at the black body of the switch, its just an info which color the led has.

### **Modification Noise Gain**

i installed a 50K Trimmer in the noise output path,

just scratch/destroy the trace and solder a 50k trimmer between the 2 Vias.

you can simply use 2 resistor legs and put them thru the Vias and solder from the Pan solder side a trimmer.
