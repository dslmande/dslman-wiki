# Roland Jupiter4 Repair and Service

1. I got a defect JP4 rev.D for repair, one voice VCO1 wasnt working correctly. after 3 hours it was back to work.
2. a full repap of all PCBs was required due to the age.
3. removing of the battery
4. cleaning of the sliders/Faders and lubrication
5. install of the hamburger MIDI Interface
6. test and calibration
