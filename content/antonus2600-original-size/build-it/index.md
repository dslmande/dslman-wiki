### **Power wiring:**

Transformer wiring: 

![IMG_7138.jpeg](assets/IMG_7138.jpeg)

maybe you have to use 2k epoxy glue to mount the IEC inlet (was needed for my IEC model)

![IMG_8983.jpeg](assets/IMG_8983.jpeg)

![IMG_8984.jpeg](assets/IMG_8984.jpeg)

I connected everything in this standard way:

IEC  P and N  (2 cables) to &gt; Frontpanel power switch &gt; 2 cables to transformer PCB input

IEC earth to transformer and frontpanel ground 

PSU PCB output +15/0/-15V to mainpcb

PSU PCB 3x MTA100 with drilled cable to the VCOS ! important

PSU PCB MTA156 2pole to Amplifier (double check the polarity)
