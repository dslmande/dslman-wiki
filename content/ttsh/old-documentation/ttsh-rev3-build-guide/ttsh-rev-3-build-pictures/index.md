### 4012 Filter

![4012-blank.jpg](assets/4012-blank.jpg)

![4012_filter.JPG](assets/4012_filter.jpg)

 

Notice the LM301 is soldered directly to the pcb. However you should use IC sockets.

There are 2 things to keep in mind:

<details>

![4012_bc558b.JPG](assets/4012_bc558b.jpg)

![4012_3958.JPG](assets/4012_3958.jpg)
