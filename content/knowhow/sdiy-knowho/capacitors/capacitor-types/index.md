# capacitor types

this pages gives you an overview on many different capacitor types

Here is a comprehensive list of various capacitor types:

1. **Ceramic Capacitors**
  - Class 1 (NP0/C0G)
  - Class 2 (X7R, X5R, Y5V)
2. **Electrolytic Capacitors**
  - Aluminum Electrolytic Capacitors
  - Tantalum Electrolytic Capacitors
  - Niobium Electrolytic Capacitors
3. **Film Capacitors**
  - Polyester Film Capacitors
  - Polypropylene Film Capacitors
  - Polycarbonate Film Capacitors
  - Polystyrene Film Capacitors
  - Paper Film Capacitors
4. **Supercapacitors (Ultracapacitors)**
  - Electric Double-Layer Capacitors (EDLC)
  - Pseudocapacitors
5. **Mica Capacitors**
  - Silver Mica Capacitors
  - Glass Mica Capacitors
6. **Tantalum Capacitors**
  - Solid Tantalum Capacitors
  - Tantalum Polymer Capacitors
7. **Aluminum Capacitors**
  - Standard Aluminum Capacitors
  - Low-ESR Aluminum Capacitors
8. **Variable Capacitors**
  - Air Variable Capacitors
  - Trimmer Capacitors
  - Ganged Variable Capacitors
9. **Integrated Capacitors**
  - Capacitor Arrays
  - Capacitor Networks
10. **Hybrid Capacitors**
  - Combination of different capacitor technologies
11. **Non-polarized Capacitors**
  - Used in AC applications
12. **High Voltage Capacitors**
  - Designed for high voltage applications
13. **Low Voltage Capacitors**
  - Suitable for low voltage applications
14. **SMD Capacitors (Surface Mount Device)**
  - SMD Ceramic Capacitors
  - SMD Tantalum Capacitors
15. **Power Capacitors**
  - Used in power factor correction
16. **Pulse Capacitors**
  - Designed for high-frequency applications
17. **RF Capacitors**
  - Used in radio frequency applications
18. **Automotive Capacitors**
  - Designed for automotive applications
19. **Aerospace Capacitors**
  - Designed for aerospace applications
20. **High Temperature Capacitors**
  - Suitable for high-temperature environments

This list covers a wide range of capacitor types used in various applications across different industries.
