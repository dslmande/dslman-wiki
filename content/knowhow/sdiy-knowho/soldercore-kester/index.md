# Soldercore Kester

## Solder Core Versions

When working with soldering in electronics, two main types of solder core are commonly used:

1. **Water Soluble Solder Core**
  - This type is ideal for components that may come into contact with water, such as resistors, capacitors, and IC sockets.
  - **Part Number**: 24-6337-6403
  - **Link**: [Kester Water Soluble Solder](https://www.digikey.de/de/products/detail/kester-solder/24-6337-6403/6048)
2. **No Clean Solder Core**
  - This solder core is used for components that will be installed after washing the PCBs with hot water, such as trimmers and potentiometers.
  - **Part Number**: 24-6337-8809
  - **Link**: [Kester No Clean Solder](https://www.digikey.de/de/products/detail/kester-solder/24-6337-8809/365521?s=N4IgTCBcDa4CwFoBsBmFB2BAOLAGAnCALoC%2BQA)

These solder cores are essential for ensuring the reliability and functionality of electronic assemblies, depending on the specific requirements of the components being used.
